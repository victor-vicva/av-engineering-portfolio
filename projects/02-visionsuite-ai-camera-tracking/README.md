# Meeting room with AI camera tracking (Q-SYS VisionSuite)

**Client:** corporate client. Name withheld.
**Role:** AV Project Engineer: design, system configuration, UCI control panel, commissioning.

## Brief

A meeting room where the camera follows the active speaker automatically, so remote participants always see who is talking without anyone operating the camera.

## Solution

- Q-SYS VisionSuite with AI-based automatic camera tracking
- Tracking driven by **microphone position data** combined with the **main camera**
- Room control through a Q-SYS UCI panel (configured as part of the project)

## Delivery

- System design and configuration
- UCI control panel
- Commissioning and user handover
