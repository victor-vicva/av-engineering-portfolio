## What I do

- **Design:** needs analysis, system architecture, AutoCAD drawings, schematics and rack elevations.
- **Control and DSP:** Q-SYS (UCI, VisionSuite, Lua scripting), Crestron, Biamp Tesira, BSS.
- **Video conferencing:** Cisco Room Kit EQ, Microsoft Teams Rooms, Logitech Rally.
- **AV over IP:** Dante, AES67, Crestron DM NVX, coordination with corporate IT networks.
- **Video and LED:** LED walls (NovaStar, Absen, Samsung), video processing, digital signage.
- **Delivery and operations:** commissioning, quality control, user training, documentation, vendor management, support and preventive maintenance.
